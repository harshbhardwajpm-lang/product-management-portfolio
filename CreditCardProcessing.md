# Product Requirements Document (PRD) – Credit Card Processing (DES Team)

1. Objective
Deliver a compliant and efficient credit card transaction processing solution within the DES platform, ensuring accuracy, scalability, and adherence to PCI DSS, and GDPR regulations.

2. Background
The DES team manages daily, weekly, and monthly transaction feeds for multiple banks. Current manual reconciliation processes lead to errors and delays, impacting settlement timelines and compliance reporting.

3. Scope
- Automate reconciliation workflows to reduce manual intervention.
- Build compliance dashboards for regulatory reporting.
- Implement audit-ready logs for transparency and faster reviews.
- Ensure integration with Visa NDM and SFTP channels.

4. Key Features
- Automation Scripts: Reduce reconciliation errors by 15%.
- Compliance Dashboards: mprove settlement timelines by 20%.
- Audit Logs: Cut compliance review time by 25%.
- Scalability: Handle 1M+ monthly transactions across regions.

 5. Stakeholders
- Product Owner / Business Analyst (requirements, backlog prioritization)
- Engineering Team (development, integration)
- Compliance & Legal (regulatory validation)
- Client Banks (UAT, feedback)

 6. Success Metrics
- Error reduction percentage in reconciliation.
- Settlement timeline improvement.
- Compliance review cycle time reduction.
- Stakeholder satisfaction scores.

 7. Risks & Mitigation
- Regulatory changes → Continuous monitoring and agile backlog updates.
- Cross-regional conflicts → Business value scoring to prioritize features.
- Integration delays → Early technical feasibility checks.

 8. Timeline
- Sprint 1: Automation scripts
- Sprint 2: Compliance dashboards
- Sprint 3: Audit logs & KPI dashboard
