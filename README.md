# Product Management Portfolio – Credit Card Processing (DES Team)

## Role & Responsibilities
As a Product Owner / Business Analyst in the Data Exchange (DES) team:
- Defined product requirements for credit card transaction reconciliation and compliance reporting.
- Collaborated with cross-functional teams (engineering, QA, compliance, client banks).
- Managed backlog prioritization using business value scoring.
- Delivered features under tight regulatory deadlines (PCI DSS, GDPR).

## Key Outcomes
- Reduced reconciliation errors by **15%** through automation scripts.
- Improved settlement timelines by **20%** with compliance dashboards.
- Cut compliance review time by **25%** using AI-driven audit-ready logs.
- Enhanced stakeholder satisfaction by aligning India and US priorities.
